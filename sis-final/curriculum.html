Educational Philosophy

Learning at your own pace.

The SOT (School of Tomorrow) system is built on the belief that students learn best when they progress through mastery — not just time spent. Every student sets daily goals, works through structured modules, and advances only after genuinely understanding the material.

Self-Directed Learning

Students take ownership of their academic progress through daily goal-setting and self-paced PACE workbooks. Teachers serve as supervisors and mentors rather than lecturers.

Mastery-Based Progression

No student moves forward until they have demonstrated genuine understanding. This approach builds lasting knowledge, not short-term performance for tests.

Character Formation

Academic rigor is inseparable from character development. Scripture memory, ethical reasoning, and service are woven throughout every year of study.

Global Readiness

Graduates are prepared for university admission worldwide. Our curriculum aligns with international standards while developing the critical thinking that leading institutions demand.

Academic Departments

Eight departments.
One integrated vision.

Each department offers a coherent, sequenced program of study — from foundational skills in the lower school to advanced coursework and independent research in the upper school.

Mathematics
Core · Advanced · Applied

From arithmetic foundations through calculus and statistics. Students develop both computational fluency and the ability to reason mathematically across real-world contexts.

Sciences
Biology · Chemistry · Physics

Laboratory-centered inquiry from the elementary years through upper school advanced courses. Students develop scientific literacy, experimental design skills, and evidence-based reasoning.

English & Literature
Language Arts · Composition · AP English

Reading widely, writing clearly, thinking critically. Students engage with literature from multiple traditions and develop the academic writing skills essential for university study.

History & Social Science
World History · Korean History · AP

Understanding the past to navigate the present. Students study Korean, Asian, and global history with attention to primary sources, historical thinking, and civic responsibility.

World Languages
Korean · Mandarin · Spanish

Language learning as a gateway to culture and connection. EAL support is available for international students, and native Korean speakers deepen their academic language proficiency.

Computer Science
Coding · AI Fundamentals · Robotics

Digital literacy, programming, and computational thinking from foundational concepts to advanced projects. Students build real software and engage with the ethics of technology.

Performing Arts
Theater · Music · Dance

Performance as a discipline. Students study voice, movement, and ensemble collaboration — culminating in annual productions and the Spring Showcase.

Visual Arts & Film
Studio Art · Photography · Film

From drawing fundamentals to digital photography and short film production. The annual Westflix Film Night celebrates the work of student filmmakers across all grades.
